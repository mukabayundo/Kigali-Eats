🍟 Mrs. Chips

Kigali's Favorite Spot for Crispy Fries, Juicy Burgers & Grilled Chicken

About Mrs. Chips

Mrs. Chips is one of Kigali's most beloved fast-food chains, famous for their perfectly crispy fries, flavorful burgers, and delicious grilled chicken. With multiple locations across the city, it's a go-to spot for quick, affordable, and tasty meals.

🍔 Popular Menu Items

  • Classic Fries - Golden crispy fries with special seasoning
  • Grilled Chicken - Marinated chicken with spicy or mild sauce
  • Beef Burger - Juicy beef patty with fresh vegetables
  • Chicken Burger - Grilled chicken breast with mayo
  • Chicken Wings - Crispy wings with dipping sauce
  • Fish & Chips - Fried fish fillet with fries

🌟 Customer Favorites

  1. Large Fries with Extra Seasoning
  2. Grilled Chicken Quarter with Spicy Sauce
  3. Double Beef Burger with Cheese
  4. Chicken Wings Bucket (Family Size)
  5. Fish & Chips Combo
